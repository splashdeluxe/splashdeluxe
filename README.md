## What I am working on

**[ai-job-search-template](https://github.com/splashdeluxe/ai-job-search-template)**
A job search system that runs with Muse on the free plan. It sources roles from official careers boards only, scores fit and gaps, builds one tailored CV per role and submits once, with a feedback loop on rejections.

**[open-ats-jobs](https://github.com/splashdeluxe/open-ats-jobs)**
Scrapes job listings straight from company career sites across 10 ATS platforms, normalised into one schema. Free expired-job detection.

**[Risk Radar](https://github.com/splashdeluxe/risk-radar)**
AI-assisted delivery-risk dashboard for Scrum and SAFe teams. Scores risks by probability and impact, routes low-confidence AI flags to a human review queue.
